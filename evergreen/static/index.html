PLANNING ASSUMPTIONS
View calculation assumptions

Constant travel speed: 0.8 m/s. Elevator delays come from the route data. Stairs and inaccessible links are excluded.

Transfer estimates include a return after each cart trip and 2 minutes of handling per computer. Installation, testing and the initial trip from the office are excluded.
